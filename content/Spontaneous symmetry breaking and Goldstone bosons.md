# Goldstone boson
We have a field with Lagrangian of form 
$$
\mathcal{L}=\partial_{\mu}{\phi}\partial_{\mu}{\bar{\phi}} - V(\phi),
$$
$$
V=\frac{1}{2}(|\phi^{2}|-\mu^{2})^{2}, \ \mu^{2} >0.
$$

Lets introduce a transformation
$$
\phi\to \exp\left\{i X \right\}\phi.
$$
$$
\phi(x)=(\mu+H(x))e^{i\theta(x)}, \ H(x)\ll1
$$
$$
\partial_{\mu}{\phi}=[\partial_{\mu}{H}+i\partial_{\mu}{\theta(\mu+H)}]e^{i\theta}
$$
$$
\mathcal{L}=\partial_{\mu}{H}\partial^{\mu}{H}+\underbrace{ (\mu+H)^{2}\partial_{\mu}{\theta}\partial^{\mu}{\theta} }_{ \text{Goldstone boson }}-V(H)
$$
New field has no mass term.
