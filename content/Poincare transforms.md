Poincare transforms are transforms under which Minkovski metric remains  invariant:

$$
x'^{\mu}=L^{\mu}_{\ \ \ \nu}x^{\nu}+a^{\mu },\ \ (1)
$$
$$
\frac{ \partial x'^{\mu} }{ \partial x^{\rho}}\frac{ \partial x'^{\nu} }{ \partial x^{\lambda} } \eta_{\mu \nu}=\eta_{\rho\lambda} \ \ (2) 
$$
From (1) and (2):
$$
L^{\mu}_{\ \ \ \rho}L^{\nu}_{\ \ \ \lambda}g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\rho\lambda}.\ (3)
$$
