Chirality is an eigenvalue of $\gamma^{5}$.
It is Lorentz-invariant, but not conserved.

For high energies [[Chirality]] and [[Helicity]] are the same. This can be shown by acting with $\gamma^{5}$ on the solutions of [[Dirac equation]] when $E \to \infty$.
