Lorentz transforms are a subset of [[Poincare transforms]] of the form
$$
x'^{\mu}=L^{\mu}_{\ \ \ \nu}x^{\nu}, \ \ \ (1)
$$
where
$$
L^{\mu}_{\ \ \ \rho}L^{\nu}_{\ \ \ \lambda}g_{\mu\nu}=g_{\rho\lambda}. \ \ \ (2)
$$
We can also write $(2)$ as 
$$
\hat{L}^{T}\eta \hat{L}=\eta. \ \ \ (3)
$$
