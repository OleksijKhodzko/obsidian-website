By definition, delta function is a function, which is just $1$ in phase space: $\tilde{\delta}(k)\equiv 1$
$$
\delta(x)=\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{4}}\int d^{4}k\ e^{ ikx }.
$$
We define [[Fourier transform]] with $2\pi$ factor so that [[Delta function]] is normalized to 1. One can check that
$$
1 = \int d^{4}x\  \left(\frac{1}{(2\pi)^{4}}\int d^{4}k\ e^{ ikx } \right).
$$
