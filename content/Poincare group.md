# Subsets of Poincare group
Translations in Minkowski space-time are a [[normal subgroup]] of Poincare group.
Poincare group is a [[semidirect product]] of this translations and proper orthochronous Lorentz group: $\mathcal{P} = I_{4} \rtimes \mathcal{L}_{+}^{\uparrow}$.
