Полярні координати
---
Запишемо [[Якобіан|якобіан]] для полярних координат:
$$\large\displaystyle 
|J| = \begin{vmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial{x}}{\partial{\rho}} & \dfrac{\partial{x}}{\partial{\phi}} \\
\dfrac{\partial{y}}{\partial{\rho}} & \dfrac{\partial{y}}{\partial{\phi}} \\
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
\cos\phi & -\rho\sin\phi \\
\sin\phi & \rho\cos\phi \\
\end{vmatrix} = \rho
$$
$\large\displaystyle  \iint\limits_{D} f(x,y)dxdy = \iint\limits_{D} f(\rho\cos\phi, \rho\sin\phi)\rho d\rho d\phi$

Розширені полярні координати
---
$$\large\displaystyle 
|J| = \begin{vmatrix}
\dfrac{\partial{x}}{\partial{\rho}} & \dfrac{\partial{x}}{\partial{\phi}} \\
\dfrac{\partial{y}}{\partial{\rho}} & \dfrac{\partial{y}}{\partial{\phi}} \\
\end{vmatrix} = \begin{vmatrix}
a\cos\phi & -a\rho\sin\phi \\
b\sin\phi & b\rho\cos\phi \\
\end{vmatrix} = ab\rho
$$
$\large\displaystyle  \iint\limits_{D} f(x,y)dxdy = \iint\limits_{D} f(a\rho\cos\phi, b\rho\sin\phi)ab\rho d\rho d\phi$
