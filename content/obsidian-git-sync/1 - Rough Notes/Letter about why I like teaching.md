There are two types of reasons why i like teaching: trivial and philosophical. Lets start with the first one to get a bit  eloquent in the end.

To begin with, I'd call myself an extrovert, I get satisfaction from communicating with people, which is an integral part of the teaching process. Second of all, the best way to learn something, they say, is to explain it to someone else. I, as a always-learning person, really appreciate this fact.

And usually tend to explain or just tell  someone all the new interesting things i learn.

Now, to the philosophical part. I always say that I have 3 main motivations in life (if we are not counting the life itself): love, science, and my state. Obviously, teaching is one of those things, which make science possible. New generations of physicians,  mathematics, linguists and microbiologists can only grow if someone teach them. The second core value which aligns with teaching is state. I strongly believe that the only way to a less bloody and depressive future is knowledge. Only when people get a sufficient level of education, they can develop critical thinking, get to a human-level level of life and start caring about such abstract principles as social responsibility, initiative, justice, etc. And that's the beginning of the fight for the better future.

So yes, it got quite pompous. But, in the end, i am just an always-learner, I like getting smarter every day, and I am not too greedy that I wouldn't help others like me.
