## Invariant amplitude for muon decay

If we also add [[W- boson]] propagator, we get
$$
M=\frac{G}{\sqrt{ 2 }}(\overline{u}_{\nu_{\mu}}\gamma^{5}(1-\gamma^{\mu})u_{\mu})\Delta_{M^{-}} (u_{e}\gamma^{5}(1-\gamma^{\mu})u_{\nu_{e}}),
$$
where 
$$
\Delta_{M^{-}}=\frac{g_{\mu\nu}-\frac{q_{\mu}q_{\nu}}{(Mc)^{2}}}{q^{2}-(Mc)^{2}}.
$$
