Basis for generators of $SU(2)$ group are Pauli matrices.

![[Pauli matrices]]

Elements of the group depend on 3 parameters: $U(\varepsilon_{1}, \varepsilon_{2}, \varepsilon_{3}) = \exp\left\{i\varepsilon_a\sigma_a\right\}.$

We usually use $J_i = \frac{1}{2}\sigma_i$ as generators, because the structure constant is Levi-Civita tensor then.
$$
\Big[J_a, J_b \Big] = i\varepsilon_{abc}J_c
$$
